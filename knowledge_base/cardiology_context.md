## Specialty Focus
Cardiology ward monitoring focuses on maintaining myocardial perfusion, rate/rhythm control, and systemic hemodynamic stability.

## Combined Vital Sign Patterns
- **Tachycardia (HR > 120) + Hypertension (BP > 160)**: Indicates severe sympathetic surge or hyperadrenergic crisis. High double product (HR × Systolic BP) dramatically increases myocardial oxygen demand.
- **Tachycardia (HR > 120) + Hypoxia (SpO2 < 90)**: Cardiopulmonary strain. Hypoxia induces reflex tachycardia, which in turn reduces coronary perfusion time, precipitating acute myocardial ischemia.

## Inpatient Triage & Escalation
- Immediate continuous telemetry monitoring.
- Stat 12-lead electrocardiogram (ECG).
- Troponin and cardiac biomarker sampling if chest pain or ischemia is suspected.
- Cardiologist notification for titration of beta-blockers, ACE inhibitors, or antiarrhythmics.
