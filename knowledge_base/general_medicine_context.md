## Specialty Focus
General Medicine inpatient units manage complex multi-system illness, sepsis, respiratory disease, and systemic inflammatory deterioration.

## Combined Vital Sign Patterns
- **High Temperature (> 38.5°C) + Low SpO2 (< 90%)**: High suspicion for severe pneumonia, pulmonary infection, or septic shock with pulmonary compromise.
- **High Temperature (> 38.5°C) + High HR (> 120 bpm) + Tachypnea (RR > 24/min)**: Meets 3/4 Systemic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (SIRS) criteria, indicating acute infection or emerging sepsis.

## Inpatient Triage & Escalation
- Immediate Sepsis Six protocol evaluation: oxygen administration, blood cultures, IV antibiotics, fluid resuscitation, lactate measurement, and urine output monitoring.
- Frequent vital signs reassessment (every 15 minutes).
- General Medicine attending physician escalation for antimicrobial adjustment and ICU transfer screening.
